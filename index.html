OVERVIEW

Empowering Next-Gen Innovators & Prototypers

AICTE IDEA LAB at Tulsiramji Gaikwad Patil College of Engineering & Technology (TGPCET) provides 24x7 hands-on prototyping facilities for ideation, product design, and research under expert faculty mentorship.

Vision Statement

"To establish IDEA LAB as a Centre of Excellence for innovation, design, research, and interdisciplinary learning that empowers students to transform ideas into impactful real-world solutions."

5 LAB SECTIONS

State-of-the-Art Prototyping Units

SECTION 01

SOFTWARE CELL

High-performance workstations featuring AutoCAD, Fusion 360, VS Code, SolidWorks, and simulation frameworks.

Section Head: Prof. A. K. Sharma

SECTION 02

IOT & PCB DESIGN

IoT microcontrollers, sensor kits, and automated CNC IoT PCB Design Machine for rapid double-sided PCB etching.

Section Head: Dr. R. V. Deshmukh

SECTION 03

3D PRINTING & PROTOTYPING

Features 2 dual industrial-grade 3D Printers (FDM + High Precision Resin SLA) for physical rapid modeling.

Section Head: Prof. S. N. Kulkarni

SECTION 04

ROBOTICS & AUTOMATION

Advanced robotics cell including a 6-Axis Industrial Robotic Arm, CNC Lathe, and CNC Milling Machine.

Section Head: Prof. M. B. Patil

SECTION 05

MACHINING & FABRICATION

Heavy metalworking facility including high-precision CO2 Laser Cutting Machine, CNC Router, and Lathe Machine.

Section Head: Prof. V. P. Joshi
